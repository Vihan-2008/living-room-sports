Living Room Sports

Turn on motion controls

Phones and tablets only share their motion sensors with secure pages. Install the PC's certificate on this phone once, and the controller uses motion from then on.

Open this page on the phone or iPad you will play with.

  1. Download the certificate. When Safari asks about a configuration profile, tap Allow.

    Download the certificate

  2. Open Settings and tap Profile Downloaded near the top. Tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install on each screen that follows.

  3. In Settings, go to General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and turn on Living Room Sports local CA.

  4. Come back to this page. It checks again by itself.

  1. Download the certificate. It is saved to Downloads.

    Download the certificate

  2. Open Settings, search for CA certificate and open it. Tap Install anyway, then choose LivingRoomSports.crt.

    On Samsung phones and tablets it is under Security and privacy › More security settings › Install from device storage. A device without a screen lock asks you to set one first.

  3. Come back to this page. It checks again by itself.

    Use Chrome or Samsung Internet. Firefox ignores certificates installed in Settings.

About this certificate

Living Room Sports made this certificate on your PC. It only vouches for addresses on private networks such as your home Wi-Fi, so it cannot be used to pose as a website on the internet.

To remove it from an iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management. On Android, search Settings for User credentials.
